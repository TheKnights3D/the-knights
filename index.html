FAQ

Common questions.

How is the price calculated?

$10 per hour of print time, billed by the minute, with a $3 minimum per order, based on the slicer estimate we share with you before printing. Standard PLA filament is included in that rate.

What files do you accept?

STL, 3MF, and OBJ work best. No file? Describe your idea and we'll talk options.

What materials can you print?

Standard PLA is included in the $10/hour rate. Ask in your request if you need a specific color.

How long does it take?

Depends on print time and the queue. We'll give you an estimated ready date with your quote.

Do you ship?

Yes. Local pickup in Murray, UT is free. Shipping is extra and quoted separately based on size and destination. The exact pickup address in Murray, UT is emailed after your order is confirmed.
